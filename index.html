- Studied at Aarhus University for two weeks in summer 2026. Nominated and selected to study "Learn software design using C++ from the inventor of C++." I still can’t believe I had the opportunity to be taught by the inventor of C++, Dr Bjarne Stroustrup! I did pass this course whilst learning so much valuable information. I had the opportunity to develop my knowledge of C++ and software design, covering topics including algorithms, memory and performance, concurrency, parallelism and energy-efficient software. For my final project, I explored the question “Can C++ be Environmentally Efficient?”, examining how algorithmic choices, memory usage, and parallelism can affect both performance and energy consumption.
